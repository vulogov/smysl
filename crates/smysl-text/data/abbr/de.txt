# de — tokens after which a sentence boundary is suppressed. Format: see `en.txt`.
#
# German is the language where this list earns its place: `Abb. 3.`, `Vgl. S. 14 f.` and
# `Nr. 5` are all ordinary prose, and UAX #29 breaks inside the first two because an
# uppercase word follows a period. The lowercase forms are listed beside the capitalised ones
# rather than folded, for the reason `en.txt` gives.
#
# `usw.` and `etc.` are absent: both end German sentences routinely. So are `f.` and `ff.`,
# which measuring cost a boundary — "Vgl. S. 14 f. Dort steht es." is two sentences, and a
# list that holds on `f.` cannot find the second one. Before a lowercase word UAX #29 holds
# them anyway, which is the case they would have been listed for.
