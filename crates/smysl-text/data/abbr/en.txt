# en — tokens after which a sentence boundary is suppressed (SMYSL-2.4 §3.2).
#
# One token per line, `#` starts a comment, blank lines are ignored. A token is matched
# **case-sensitively** and must end in `.`, which is the whole reason the list is data: a
# case-insensitive match would fold `No.` into `no.` and join "the answer was no. Then he
# left." into one sentence. The list says `No.`; the word is left alone.
#
# What is deliberately absent: `etc.`, `al.`, `usw.` and their kin. They end sentences often
# enough that holding on them loses more boundaries than it saves, and UAX #29 already
# suppresses the break when a lowercase word follows — which is the case these would be
# listed for.
#
# Single uppercase initials (`J.`, `M.`) are a rule in `segment.rs`, not rows here: the
# alternative is 26 lines per language that say the same thing.
